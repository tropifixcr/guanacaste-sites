# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of homes, condos and vacation rentals between Tamarindo and Las Catalinas (Tamarindo, Langosta, Playa Grande, Conchal, Brasilito, Flamingo, Potrero, Las Catalinas, Huacas) and around Coco and Papagayo (Playas del Coco, Playa Hermosa, Ocotal, Playa Panamá, Papagayo, Sardinal), in Guanacaste, Costa Rica. English-speaking first: owners living here, absentee owners, vacation rental hosts and property managers. Spanish-speaking residents and businesses second.

Situation: A stain on the ceiling, water coming in during heavy rain, gutters overflowing, or a new roof for a build or renovation. Leaks are urgent in the rainy season; absentee owners need someone who can check an empty house.

## Product Purpose

Guanacaste Roofing (guanacasteroofing.com) is a single-trade lead site. Its job is to rank in Google and be quoted in AI answers for its trade in these towns, then turn a visitor into a request through the form or WhatsApp. Success is requests received. Later the site is rented to one local provider for a monthly fee, and its form and WhatsApp go straight to them.

Services: Leak finding and repair, tile and metal roof repairs, sealing and waterproof membranes, gutters and downspouts, inspection before the rainy season, new roofs and roof replacement.

## Positioning

Local and specific to coastal Guanacaste, free for the visitor, and honest that a local partner roofer does the work. A national company or directory cannot speak to these towns, this climate and these properties with the same specificity.

Local knowledge the site can use: After months of dry heat the first heavy rains find every crack. Most leaks show up in May and again in September and October. Late dry season (March, April) is the time to inspect.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google or an AI answer, mostly on a phone, often in heat or in a hurry. WhatsApp is the normal way to reach a tradesperson in Costa Rica. Loic receives every request during the test and forwards it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact: a request form (Netlify Forms) and a WhatsApp button to +506 8701 6040 (Loic's number during the test). Loic forwards each request to a local technician as a free lead. A call-tracking number may come later.
- Honest about who answers: the site is not a company. It says plainly that requests go to a local partner roofer. No time promise for the reply ("a local roofer will contact you").
- Consent (Costa Rica's Ley 8968): the form asks consent before the visitor's details are shared with a local roofer. A privacy page explains what is collected and who receives it.
- Language: English first, full Spanish version reached by a switch in the header. Spanish uses "usted". Each language written natively.
- Facts: no prices, response times, guarantees, licences, years in business or number of technicians unless Loic confirms them. Free for the visitor is confirmed.
- No testimonials, reviews, ratings, customer counts or partner logos exist; none may be invented.
- Images: generated with Higgsfield only, after Loic approves the cost; equipment, places and conditions, never a fake person, customer or finished job. Real local photos replace them later.
- Not indexed until launch (noindex and robots.txt). Runs on guanacasteroofing.netlify.app until the domain is connected.
- Performance on a phone: LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1, Lighthouse 90+. Tap targets 44px or more, body and form text 16px or more.
- No TropiFix branding. This site must look and read clearly different from its two sister sites (Guanacaste AC, Guanacaste Bug Control, Guanacaste Roofing), and must not link to them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Guanacaste Roofing. No logo exists yet; a wordmark is acceptable.
- Voice: a friendly local expert. Warm, plain and specific. No hype words, no fake urgency, no emoji.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no photos of local work, no testimonials, no reviews, no named technicians. The reference copy for this trade in `../../TropiFix/src/data/services.en.ts` and `services.es.ts` may be reused.

## Product Principles

- One job: turn a worried visitor into a request in as few steps as possible.
- Serve two visits: the urgent leak and the planned new roof, without either burying the other.
- Local over generic: town names, climate and property types, used naturally.
- Honest by default: say who answers, promise nothing unconfirmed.
- Phone first: everything that converts must work one-handed on a small screen.
