Scope: the landing page (EN at /, ES at /es/), plus thanks and privacy pages in the same world. Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: an owner whose AC is misbehaving in the heat, on a phone, wants a technician. Action: the request form or WhatsApp. Proof: plain local knowledge (salt air, cleaning cycles), no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the AC remote every owner already holds. The visitor presses the key that matches what their unit is doing, the LCD answers, and SEND opens the request. Refuses the stock contractor page of a technician photo, three service cards and an orange button.
OWN-WORLD: Off-white remote plastic ground (#EEF1EC), grey rubber keys, a recessed grey-green LCD (#B9C7B4) with dark segment ink (#1B241D), and one "cool" blue (#1D5FD0) reserved for the primary action. Seven-segment numerals (DSEG7) only on the LCD; Archivo, wide and narrow widths, for labels and text. Keys have pressed and lit states.
STORY: You recognise your remote, find your symptom as a key, read a plain answer about what it likely is, and send it to a local technician by form or WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: name bar with language switch; headline in two lines; the LCD panel showing the room temperature and "COOL"; a 2x3 grid of symptom keys; a full-width blue SEND key and a WhatsApp key beneath. Desktop: LCD and keypad as a large remote on the right, headline and short answer on the left.
