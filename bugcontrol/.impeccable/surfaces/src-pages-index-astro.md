# Home page: Guanacaste Bug Control

Scope: the landing page (EN at /, ES at /es/), plus thanks and privacy pages in the same world. Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: an owner or host who has just found termites, scorpions, ants or rodents, a little alarmed, on a phone. Action: report it through the form or WhatsApp. Proof: plain identification knowledge, no invented claims.
Unresolved: specimen illustrations from Higgsfield pending cost approval; until then each plate figure is a clearly marked placeholder.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a field guide plate of the pests you actually meet in Guanacaste. You find the one you saw, read what it means, and report it. Refuses the exterminator template of a green logo, bug icon tiles and a family photo.
OWN-WORLD: Pale sage plate paper (#DCE5D3), deep forest ink (#1C2A21), rust for figure numbers and labels (#A4471F). Bodoni Moda for headings and Latin names, Figtree for text. Specimens sit in ruled plate frames with figure numbers; selected specimen gets a rust frame.
STORY: You recognise the creature, learn the signs and whether it is urgent, and report it to a local pest technician, with the pest already filled in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: name and language switch; headline in two lines; a plate of six figures (termites, ants, scorpions, cockroaches, rodents, mosquitoes) in a 2x3 grid; a rust "Report what you found" button and WhatsApp below. Desktop: headline and short answer left, the full plate right.
FORM: Impeccable's pick, the field guide plate (grounded list position 1), chosen by Loic; seed key 9eac9fe6.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
