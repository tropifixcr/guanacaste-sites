After months of dry heat, the first heavy rains in May find every crack in a roof. Tiles shift, sealant dries out and gutters fill with leaves and dust. A little attention at the end of the dry season saves a lot of ceiling repairs later.

> **Short answer:** have the roof checked in March or April. Look for broken or slipped tiles, rusted sheets and loose screws, cracked sealant around chimneys and skylights, and gutters full of debris. Inside, look for old stains on the ceiling. Fix it all before the rains start.

## Why March and April

The rainy season in Guanacaste usually starts in May, and most leaks show up then and again in September and October, when the rain is heaviest. A roof checked at the end of the dry season can be repaired in dry weather. Once leaks start, everyone calls at the same time.

*The first heavy rains of May are the real test of a roof that has spent months in the sun.*

## A checklist from the ground and the attic

You do not need to climb on the roof to spot most problems. Walk around the house with good light, and look inside too.

- **Tiles:** any that are cracked, slipped out of line or missing.
- **Metal sheets:** rust spots, lifted edges and screws that have worked loose.
- **Flashing and sealant:** around chimneys, skylights, vents and where the roof meets a wall.
- **Ceilings inside:** brown rings or soft spots, even small and old ones. They show where water has already found a way in.
- **The attic or roof space,** if you have one: daylight showing through, or damp wood.

Please do not walk on clay tiles yourself. They break easily, and a fall from a roof is far worse than a leak.

## Gutters and downspouts

Leaves, dust and nests build up over the dry season. When the first storm comes, a blocked gutter overflows against the wall or backs up under the roof edge. Clear them, and check that each downspout carries the water well away from the house.

![A metal gutter overflowing during heavy tropical rain](../images/gutter-overflowing.png)
*An overflowing gutter pours water down the wall and can push it back under the roof edge.*

## Flat roofs and terraces

Flat roofs and roof terraces depend on a waterproof membrane, and months of strong sun crack it. Look for blisters, splits and places where water stays in puddles after you hose it down. Those are the spots that will leak first.

## When to call a roofer

- You found broken tiles, rusted sheets or cracked sealant.
- There are stains on the ceiling, even old ones.
- Gutters overflow or pull away from the roof.
- Part of the roof is out of sight or out of safe reach.
- You are planning a renovation or a new roof and want it done before the rains.
